Partnerships

Building the Right Partnership for Türkiye

OrphaRx is open to discussions with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies seeking to develop the commercial potential of specialised medicines in the Turkish market.

We are currently establishing our portfolio and are actively evaluating selected products and partnership opportunities aligned with our therapeutic focus and commercial capabilities.

Our objective is not to build a large portfolio, but to establish a focused portfolio of products where we can provide the appropriate level of attention, expertise and commercial execution.

What We Can Offer

A Focused Local Commercial Platform

For selected products, OrphaRx can provide a focused commercial platform in Türkiye built around:

Market & Product Assessment

Understanding the product, therapeutic environment, competitive dynamics and commercial opportunity.

Distribution

Establishing and managing the local distribution structure appropriate to the product.

Market Development

Developing product-specific commercial priorities based on the treatment environment and relevant healthcare stakeholders.

Sales & Marketing

Focused commercial execution across relevant physicians, treatment centres and key accounts.

Commercial Planning

Forecasting, resource planning and performance monitoring to support disciplined commercial development.

Partnership Opportunities

Depending on the product and the objectives of the pharmaceutical or biotechnology partner, potential structures may include:

Distribution Partnerships

Exclusive or appropriately structured distribution arrangements for selected products in Türkiye.

Commercialisation Partnerships

Partnerships focused on developing and executing the local commercial strategy for specialised medicines.

In-Licensing Opportunities

Selected in-licensing opportunities aligned with our therapeutic focus and commercial capabilities.

Strategic Product Partnerships

Tailored structures developed around the characteristics and requirements of individual products.

Each opportunity is assessed individually, with the partnership structure determined according to the product, market and strategic objectives of both parties.

What We Look For

We are currently evaluating opportunities within:

Rare diseases Orphan medicines Specialty pharmaceuticals Differentiated therapies addressing significant medical needs Products with a clearly defined treatment environment Products requiring focused commercial development in Türkiye

We assess opportunities based on therapeutic relevance, product characteristics, market environment, commercial potential and strategic fit.
