Who We Are

Built on Pharmaceutical Experience

OrphaRx is a specialised pharmaceutical company focused on rare diseases, orphan medicines and specialty pharmaceuticals in Türkiye.

The company brings together professionals with experience across key commercial and operational functions of the pharmaceutical industry, including business development, commercial strategy, sales and marketing, market development and pharmaceutical operations.

Our structure is intentionally focused: rather than pursuing a broad portfolio, we aim to build a selective portfolio of specialised products where our experience and commercial capabilities can provide meaningful value.

Our Experience

Experience Across the Pharmaceutical Value Chain

Our experience extends across the commercial and operational dimensions of the pharmaceutical industry. We bring together perspectives from:

Business Development Commercial Strategy Sales & Marketing Market Development Product and Portfolio Evaluation Distribution Forecasting and Commercial Planning Pharmaceutical Operations

This cross-functional perspective allows us to evaluate opportunities beyond a single commercial function and to consider the product, market environment and execution requirements together.

Our Purpose

A Focused Approach to Specialised Medicines

Our purpose is to establish a pharmaceutical organisation capable of identifying meaningful opportunities in specialised medicine and developing them with the level of focus they require.

We believe that rare disease and specialty pharmaceuticals should be approached with a clear understanding of their therapeutic environment, patient population, healthcare stakeholders and commercial requirements.

OrphaRx aims to bring these perspectives together within a focused and responsible pharmaceutical structure.
