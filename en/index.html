Why Specialised Medicines Matter

For patients living with rare and serious diseases, access to appropriate treatment can depend on the availability of specialised therapies and the ability of the healthcare system to reach the right treatment pathways.

We believe pharmaceutical companies have a responsibility that extends beyond commercial performance.

Our ambition is to contribute to a pharmaceutical ecosystem in which specialised medicines can be appropriately evaluated, responsibly commercialised and made available within the relevant healthcare environment.
