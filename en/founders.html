Our Founders

OrphaRx brings together professionals with backgrounds spanning pharmaceuticals, healthcare, business and senior management.

The founding team combines medical, commercial, technical and corporate experience relevant to pharmaceutical opportunities in Türkiye.

ZA

Ziya Akkurt

Co-Founder

With a career spanning more than four decades, Ziya Akkurt has held senior leadership and board positions across banking, investment banking and different industries in Türkiye and international markets.

He began his banking career in 1983, progressing through senior positions at leading Turkish and international banks and serving as Chief Executive Officer of a prominent Turkish bank. His international responsibilities later included executive and board positions within Akbank subsidiaries in Germany, Malta, the Netherlands and the United Arab Emirates.

Since 2012, he has been Chairman of the Board of Nurol Investment Bank. His wider career includes senior management and board positions in manufacturing and other industries. In 2018–2019, he also served as a Board Member in an IFC-related project in Saudi Arabia.

For OrphaRx, this background provides a senior corporate perspective across strategic decision-making, governance and international business.

TY

Tuna Yavuz, M.D.

Co-Founder

Dr. Tuna Yavuz has built a career at the intersection of medicine, pharmaceuticals and healthcare management, with nearly three decades in the sector.

Following his graduation from Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine, he pursued further studies in monoclonal antibodies at the University of Manchester in the United Kingdom. His pharmaceutical career began at Sanofi Pasteur, where he served as Medical and Marketing Director.

He subsequently moved into senior management roles at Abdi İbrahim, with responsibilities in specialty pharmaceuticals, before becoming General Manager of AI Otsuka. His career also includes senior positions at Danone Türkiye and Onfarma.

This combination of medical training, pharmaceutical experience and commercial management forms an important part of the perspective he brings to OrphaRx and its assessment of opportunities in Türkiye.

FT

Fatma Tartuk

Co-Founder

Fatma Tartuk has more than 14 years of pharmaceutical experience, moving from analytical research and quality control into business development and pharmaceutical project management.

Her technical background includes analytical method development, validation, stability studies and CTD documentation. In business development, her work has covered product development, licensing, technology transfer, contract manufacturing and commercial launch planning.

She has worked across the pharmaceutical product lifecycle, coordinating activities involving R&D, regulatory, manufacturing and commercial functions. Her experience also includes TÜBİTAK-funded R&D projects and CMO coordination.

At OrphaRx, this combination of technical and business development experience is applied to the evaluation and development of niche and orphan pharmaceutical opportunities for the Turkish market.

UI

Uğur Işıklar, M.D.

Co-Founder

Dr. Uğur Işıklar is a Professor of Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology with more than three decades of clinical experience in Türkiye and advanced subspecialty training in the United States.

He graduated from Hacettepe University School of Medicine in 1985 and completed his postgraduate training in Orthopedic Surgery at Ankara University. He subsequently completed advanced fellowship training at Baylor College of Medicine in Houston in Joint Replacement, Orthopedic Trauma and Adult Reconstructive Surgery, followed by a Sports Medicine Visiting Scholar program.

His clinical career in Türkiye has included positions at Başkent University School of Medicine, the Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology Center in Ankara, Florence Nightingale Hospital and Bilim University School of Medicine in Istanbul. He later served as Chairman of Orthopedic Surgery at Şişli Memorial Hospital from 2017 to 2020. Since 2020, he has continued his clinical practice in Istanbul.

Dr. Işıklar has been involved in a number of national and international orthopedic organizations, including the Turkish Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology Board, Turkish Arthroplasty Association, AO/ASIF, SICOT, EFORT and the European Hip Society. He is a founding member of the Turkish Arthroplasty Association and has served as Chairman of the AO Chapter Türkiye and as an AO Trustee.

Levent, Istanbul, Türkiye
